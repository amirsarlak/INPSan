# ADR-0013 — Knowledge-Based and Evidence-Driven Product Engineering

Status: **ACCEPTED**  
Date: 2026-09-28

## Decision

INPSan feature priority and acceptance will be governed by operational value, proprietary technical depth, measurable complexity, security, demonstrability and reproducible evidence.

Only implemented/tested capabilities may be represented as current product technology. Planned R&D remains explicitly labeled as planned.

## Consequences

- every proprietary module requires ownership/dependency boundaries;
- every major claim requires demo/test evidence;
- complexity/performance/security metrics become part of Definition of Done;
- upstream capabilities are never presented as proprietary INPSan invention;
- UI/UX design rationale is retained as an engineering artifact;
- documentation and evidence are developed with the feature, not after it.
