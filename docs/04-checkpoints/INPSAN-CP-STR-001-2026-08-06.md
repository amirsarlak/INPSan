## Strategic decision

INPSan is re-governed through two macro stages:

1. **Stage 1 — Secure Single-Node Productization and General Availability**;
2. **Stage 2 — Enterprise Scale, Automation and Ecosystem**.

The former six-phase roadmap remains valid as technical workstreams but is superseded as the top-level delivery model.

## Why this decision is appropriate now

- the storage, telemetry, dashboard, hardware-health and alerting foundation is already mature;
- restarting or discarding completed work would be incorrect;
- the major gap is no longer basic monitoring—it is secure productization, supportability, licensing and independence;
- a clear Single-Node GA gate prevents premature multi-node, AI and hybrid-cloud expansion;
- the two-stage model creates a commercially meaningful delivery boundary similar to the governance approach used in INP DataGuard while preserving INPSan-specific architecture.

## Current maturity statement

- Existing technical foundation: approximately `90–95%` for the original operational-core scope.
- Rebaselined Stage 1 productization: approximately `55–65%` because API/RBAC/audit, full hardening, licensing, upgrade/restore and independent control-plane work remain incomplete.

This distinction is mandatory in future status reporting.

## Stage 1 mandatory outcomes

- close v3.2.4.3 operational and browser validation;
- complete disk-to-bay removal/reinsert/addition/failure tests;
- resolve NTP health;
- implement independent API, authentication, RBAC, audit, reports and scheduler;
- establish cross-cutting hardening aligned to OmniOS guidance, tailored CIS Solaris references, NIST controls/SSDF and OWASP ASVS 5.0;
- resolve napp-it bundling/redistribution dependency;
- implement safe offline-capable INPSan licensing;
- prove non-destructive license expiry;
- produce SBOM, third-party notices, signed releases, upgrade/rollback and restore evidence;
- issue Single-Node GA checkpoint.

## Stage 2 mandatory outcomes

- central manager and secure node enrollment;
- multi-node/multi-site hierarchy and federated monitoring;
- automation, orchestration and policy engine;
- replication/resilience workflows;
- analytics and prediction after data-quality gates;
- virtualization, backup and hybrid-cloud integrations.

## Security decisions locked by this checkpoint

1. hardening is continuous across both stages;
2. r151054 LTS remains the production baseline until controlled compatibility testing approves another OmniOS release;
3. CIS Oracle Solaris controls are tailoring references, not an automatic OmniOS script;
4. OWASP ASVS 5.0 Level 2 is the default Web/API target, with selected Level 3 controls for destructive administration;
5. notification channels remain disabled until channel-specific security and delivery acceptance;
6. secrets, signing keys and sensitive evidence must not be stored in the public repository.

## Licensing decisions locked by this checkpoint

1. technical independence from napp-it is the preferred route;
2. redistribution or preconfigured delivery using napp-it requires a written commercial bundling agreement;
3. all shipped components require an SBOM and recorded license;
4. license verification must be signed and offline-capable;
5. expiry must not stop existing I/O, offline LUNs/shares, block reads, delete configuration or prevent recovery;
6. fragile MAC-only binding is rejected for the final INPSan license model;
7. legal review is required before commercial distribution.
